Apple'ın ürünü.
TROY'un hizmeti.

A design system for the TROY app, built from what troyestore.com actually is: Apple's catalogue wrapped in a Turkish service layer — taksit, DeğişTokuş, mağazalar, teknik servis, TROY CLUB. Product surfaces stay quiet and Apple-adjacent. Service surfaces are where TROY gets a voice of its own.

The read

What the site gave us

Measured live: SF Pro Text throughout, #0071E3 pill buttons at 42px radius, #121212 text at 75% opacity, grey bands #F2F2F2, 8/16px card radii, a deep blue announcement rail, and a lime highlight behind campaign headlines.

What it was missing

Visually the site is Apple's system re-hosted. In an app that reads as a knock-off — and it spends nothing on the four things TROY alone owns: financing, trade-in, physical stores and Club. This system keeps Apple's calm on product, and gives TROY navy, volt and density everywhere else.

Para & Dil — Money & language

Formatting

Locale tr-TR. Thousands ., decimals ,, symbol trailing: 108.999,00 ₺. Percent sign leads the number: -%9. Everything tabular. Prices are never abbreviated.

Fixed strings

Satın Al · Gelince Haber Ver · Sepete Ekle · Stokta · Tükendi · Mağazada var · vade farksız · DeğişTokuş · Eğitime özel · TROY CLUB · Randevu Al. vade farksız is never rendered as "faizsiz".

Kurallar — Review gates

Do

Cover the logo: a product screen should look like good Apple retail; a service screen should be unmistakably TROY.

Don't

Put two volt elements in one viewport. Two offers means no offer.

Do

Ship the taksit line with every price, at every size, without a tap.

Don't

Read a primitive from a component. Semantic tokens only — this blocks review.

Do

Let stock state change the card's shape — pill, opacity and CTA move together.

Don't

Truncate a price or an installment line at 200% Dynamic Type. Reflow instead.
